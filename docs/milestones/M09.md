## Implemented behavior

Versioned, immutable audience, writing, locale and translation-route profiles carry editable reading level, vocabulary, length, tone, situation/example/action/reflection, sensitive topics, interaction/privacy limits and learning goals. Twelve explicit Draft proposals include 8–14, 15–23 and adult audiences, 15–18 and 19–23 writing subprofiles, English/Marathi/Hindi locale proposals and two routes. Profile creation and repeatable proposals are scoped and idempotent; proposing a recognized new locale adds proposed catalog data without application rebuild or implied support. Writing bands are editorial rules, separate from legal consent.

A shared bundle pins an approved exact M05 English quotation/translation and a separately attributed approved interpretation supporting the same imported verse/snapshot, learning goals, names, concept identifiers, source citations and optional retrieved canonical Sanskrit. The six sections preserve source/story/interpretation distinctions. Audience treatments are owned editable prose, not live AI generation. Meaning stays exact for English; foreign-language bodies require exact reviewed translation memory. Sanskrit is never regenerated or replaced.

ITranslationProvider and ITranslationAdapter own bounded canonical sections, configured locale terminology/transliteration/pronunciation rules, memory and route selection. reviewed-memory.a.v1 and reviewed-memory.b.v1 return only exact local approved memory; unsupported locale/treatment fails explicitly. Route selection is configuration and old variants retain original profiles, route, memory, source and cast. No vendor SDK, translation API, network, credentials, paid calls or hidden retry was added. Human review is still required for linguistic/theological meaning and pronunciation; adapter availability does not qualify a language.

Each adaptation starts its own Draft linked revision. Life-stage and optional character allowlists select a small active fictional cast from owned data. An eligible reviewed guest can be activated and selected without rebuilding; duplicate-key re-aging is rejected and previous casts remain frozen. Full registry roles/relationships/assets and promotion/retirement belong to M10/M16. No named seed is forced into every lesson.

Minimal child data, no persistent conversation profile, behavioral advertising or personalization are enforced for the current capability. Sensitive-topic and forbidden-phrase gates, preserved goal/concept identifiers and configured length bounds apply before acceptance and review. These conservative lexical checks supplement human suitability review; they do not claim universal classification.

Current evidence approval/corrections, corpus review and source rights are rechecked when bundling, adapting, reviewing, inspecting and exporting. Revocation withholds derived sections and source-bearing snapshots/citations. Actual development M04 read-only permissions remain unchanged. Export requires manual human qualification of claims, profiles, route, variant and any translation memory plus current export rights; fixture approvals never authorize release. Export is not publication.
