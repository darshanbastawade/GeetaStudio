## Implemented behavior

The staff workbench at /staff/editorial supports owned briefs/drafts, evaluation binding, review, approval, scheduling, explicitly local dry-run publication, correction/withdrawal, rejection and failure. Current and previous text, citations/disagreements, lesson variants, registry episodes, assets/checksums, AI route/prompt/model/cost and actual M12 findings appear together with lineage links and immutable action history.

Approval binds the exact revision, asset manifest, current source permissions/evidence, policies, audience, evaluation reviews, production/cast and registry versions. Text or audio changes expire it. Current direct-episode cast eligibility also blocks reapproval of retired, changed or unapproved profiles. Legacy approval/export paths cannot bypass managed content or its managed ancestors. Current rights are rechecked; source-bearing output and private asset previews are withheld when reading rights are unavailable.

Author edits their own work; Editor approves/corrects; Publisher schedules/records dry-run copies and external remediation; Administrator controls owned provider routes. Actual tenant membership and HTTP roles both apply. Scoped operation replay, rowversion conflicts, audit and immutable approvals/history/attempts persist.

Corrections traverse articles, translations, episodes, exports and posts. Withdraw old content or supply an independently reviewed replacement when immutable old ancestry remains invalid. Replacement IDs and every external Attempted/Unknown/Unsupported/Confirmed outcome are retained; genuine manual evidence is required for confirmation. No platform action is simulated as successful.

Existing M10 /staff/characters provides guest/profile/alias/preview/casting/lifecycle/continuity administration. The bounded manual trend queue shows event evidence, teaching match, freshness, transcript, proposed cast/script and cost; missing evidence or inappropriate cast blocks approval. No automatic M15 ingestion is added.

Safe provider UI validates typed routes, displays qualification and current-head versions, activates/rolls back with concurrency checks, and disables owned providers. Credential availability is a boolean; references/values are not returned. Minimal prerequisites: M07 activation respects an owning editorial transaction; M12 InspectAsync reads one actual scoped report instead of rescanning unrelated reports. The broad regression exposed a M12 privacy false positive on twelve numeric digits inside a declared citation GUID. Only valid declared GUID markers are excluded from prose privacy scanning; identifiers/emails in prose and invalid markers remain blocked, with a new offline regression.
