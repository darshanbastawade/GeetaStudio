## Behavior and boundaries

TALK uses the existing customer authority, audience/locale and approved fictional persona records. Each session binds its internal owner, profile and immutable policy revision. Current tenant, household membership, profile state, persona approval/lifecycle and audience eligibility are checked. Minor interaction counts cannot exceed the reviewed audience policy.

Learning turns reserve M20 credits before reading sources, retrieve through M6, validate current evidence and generation/AI rights through M3, pin a qualified M7 text route, reserve the M7 monetary budget, then validate the answer's citations and exact quoted passage. Unfunded requests receive no source text. Unsupported questions or contradictory generated wording return uncertainty. The launch is English and narrowly extractive: cited scripture/translation stays distinct from the fictional character's invitation to reflect. Canonical text is never fabricated or relabeled as generated authority.

Professional advice, factual news, crisis and account/tool-injection questions have controlled safe responses or escalation guidance without generation. No conversation tools can edit entitlements, select arbitrary destinations, expose credentials or retrieve another person's context.

Default lifetime is 30 minutes, up to 12 turns, with at most three active sessions/account and one credit per accepted supported turn. Configuration bounds lifetime to 5–60 minutes, turns to 1–24, and adult persistent memory to at most 24 hours. Three previous supported turns form transient context within this exact session and process. Current evidence rights are rechecked before using prior context. Restart, cancellation, deletion and expiry clear transient context.

Question/answer text is absent from default SQL and AI diagnostics. Only adults with current explicit personalization consent and session opt-in can retain conversation text. Persistent child memory and advertising are disabled. Withdrawal/profile deletion erase text in the customer transaction; expiry/current consent are checked on reads and through a bounded retention sweep. Metadata needed for original accounting, canonical evidence and reports remains. Jurisdictional retention and production privacy qualification are pending.

Cancellation shares the AI-admission SQL fence with the session/turn lease, and a scoped monitor propagates cancellation to claimed provider work. Unknown usage holds both provider budget and customer credits; it blocks further turns until original usage is reconciled. Interrupted unadmitted work can release its reservation after cancellation/expiry. Reconciliation never resubmits an unknown run.

The route is pinned after scoped retrieval and before provider admission. Changes affect new turns. Prior run/attempt/route/source and owned executor receipt identifiers remain auditable. Accepted supported output consumes the fixed customer units once. Known failures release credits while real known provider costs remain recorded; unknown outcomes stay held. Replays do not generate or charge twice.

Private `/talk` and administrator `/staff/talk` pages expose memory scope, limits, cost, cited passage links, suggested reading, reports and original-usage reconciliation. Streaming buffers and validates the provider response before emitting answer chunks. Problem reports bind the turn, operation fingerprint and original evidence/run revision.
