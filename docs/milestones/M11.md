## Implemented behavior

IDraftFactory models versioned evergreen briefs/plans, journey position, actual approved chapter/verse locators, source anchors/bundles, concepts, learning goal, audience/locale, six formats, priority and generation/media/review estimates. Proposing chronological and practical-theme pilots uses selected reviewed lessons and remains Draft until explicit review. Test fixtures use exact stored Geeta/Besant/Sanskrit verses1.1/1.2, separately attributed fixture interpretation, distinct reviewed fixture treatments and a newly registered fictional learner. Fixture review proves behavior, not genuine theological/editorial quality.

The shared M10 casting service resolves roles/pins and snapshots stable profile/core IDs, reasons, roles, presentation guides, relationships, bible and continuity. Active/current approved profiles are required for M11 plans. Lead life stages and cast bounds follow the reviewed writing profile, while compatible older mentors can support them. Missing roles require M10 guest registration/activation; retired, wrong-age, foreign, incompatible relationships and unapproved media substitutions fail. No named-character switch or forced full seed cast.

Closed local-template.a.v1/b.v1 layout versions produce explanation, original story, comic script, short-video script, carousel and reflection drafts from reviewed authored sections. Canonical anchor/attributed interpretation remain visibly distinct from fiction; canonical Sanskrit is pinned, not regenerated. M10 agency/fiction/forbidden-attribution policy applies to directions and rendered treatment. Directions are editorial annotations, not executed source/tool instructions or a model prompt. Templates perform no I/O or remote model calls and cost 0. Headers are staff format labels; no complete multilingual product quality claim.

Every artifact pins its immutable plan, lesson/canonical bundle/citations, cast/relationship/template and core content revision. Core provenance records actual source snapshots. Identical unchanged derivatives reuse stable IDs; explicit changed derivatives version deliberately, keeping previous snapshots. Source/audience/locale changes use a new core content family while retaining artifact lineage. Cached derivatives must satisfy revised character limits, and rejected outputs cannot silently reenter generation.

Batch admission is scoped and idempotent by business key, exact approved plan/budget and actor. Durable work rows, original deadline, bounded item/character quotas, attempt cap3, fenced30-second leases, tenant admission locks and atomic local-render/artifact/core/provenance/checkpoint commits support pause/resume, cancellation and crash recovery. Current state/cast/rights is checked before rendering. Cancellation/deadline closes unfinished work without accepting new output after the fence; accepted output persists. No external effect/retry/charge is hidden behind this local capability.

Exact body hashes and bounded token-set overlap detect template/near duplicates within audience/locale/format. Linked formats are intentional derivatives; deliberate revisions within a plan family are explicit. Cross-plan duplicate rejections retain work outcomes without new content. This conservative proxy is not calibrated neural semantic evaluation. Topic fatigue counts repeated learning treatments separately from their six formats; suggested priority reduces declared editorial priority for fatigue, and processing follows declared priority/journey order.

Coverage distinguishes planned, generated, reviewed, recorded-published, currently publicly usable, reused, duplicate-rejected and withheld counts. Historical generated/publication metadata persists after source revocation; source-bearing plan/artifact snapshots and unfinished generation are withheld/blocked. Published is a recorded SourceUse declaration, not independent platform-delivery verification. Current public usability additionally requires genuine manual reviews, current approved core revision, M09 export qualification, current source/M05 evidence and qualified M10 media. Draft/fixture inventory is not public readiness or demonstrated demand. No publication endpoint was added.
