## Implemented behavior

The version-one Shivaji pack defines terminology, historical methods/events/people/places, adult learning goals, source-year chronology, source/rights policies, contested accounts, legends and an original fictional archive guide. Two original secondary histories are pinned by bibliography, archived OCR hash and fourteen exact excerpt hashes: Sarkar's 1920 second edition and Kincaid/Parasnis's 1918 first volume. These are attributed historical accounts with known limitations, not neutral consensus or independently qualified primary witnesses. The source manifest records the inferred original-text rights basis and pending human redistribution/jurisdiction review; modern additions, portraits, footage and historical voice impersonation are excluded. See [source manifest](../universe-packs/shivaji/v1/sources.json), [source integrity](evidence/M26-source-checks.json) and the [1920 title/edition](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Shivaji_and_His_Times).

Ten private READ proposals cover historical method, invented guide dialogue, Torna's conjectural dating, Surat, Purandar, Agra, return chronology, coronation, a reported disagreement and a popular legend. The reported disagreement preserves Kincaid's account of Grant Duff and Kincaid's rebuttal from the same work; it does not pretend these are two independent firsthand sources. Direct Duff verification remains pending. Legends and invented dialogue are undated where no defensible date is supplied. Exact source accounts, reviewed factual paraphrases, modern interpretations and fiction retain separate evidence labels. “Documented fact” records in this private sample carry technical fixture reviews; they do not certify historical consensus or human acceptance.

Historical READ entries reuse the CMS approval, lesson, audience/locale, source-rights, correction and publication gates. They show chronology, people/places, citations, disputed accounts, legend and invented-dialogue labels through the same Blazor reader. A Windows-scoped private universe selector discovers the newest permitted collection version. Search includes historical people/place metadata. Revocation or correction of either bound disputed account hides the entry. Public activation requires genuine human-qualified inputs and remains unavailable to fixtures.

Two WATCH technical samples reuse the existing original character, approved cast/presentation, exact provider/account/profile/version/resource voice binding, installed Windows English speech, subtitle port, FFmpeg renderer, technical QA, final review and owned-master export. One reads a cited coronation claim (48.60seconds with credits); one is visibly original fictional guide dialogue (14.23seconds with credits). They retain complete credits, captions and evidence manifests; public export remains fenced. These are small technical media previews, not qualified human historical or pronunciation performances.

The reuse proof builds and shadow-validates two retrieval ranking adapters, switches one owned alias without changing source IDs/contracts, checks an exact supported excerpt and a no-match question, runs an evidence-bound zero-priced fixture workflow to AwaitingReview, creates an existing factory draft and exports a reviewed private Facebook Article package with `ExportOnly;NotPublished`.
