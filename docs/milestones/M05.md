## Implemented behavior

IEvidenceEngine owns claims, immutable versions/spans, versioned review rubrics, opposing interpretation records, content segment bindings, correction requests, supersession and impact lookup. Framework-free Domain/Contracts/Application boundaries are retained. Historical/news claims use their own types and corroboration rule; scripture certainty is never inherited. Quotation, Translation, Interpretation, ModernAnalogy, FictionalScene and Suggestion labels remain distinct, with author/tradition, uncertainty, reviewer, quality/outcome and effective version.

Evidence spans validate exact retained UTF-8 text/checksum, UTF-16 offsets and owning source scope. Quotations/translations additionally require the approved exact corpus component and immutable selected-edition snapshot/hash/locator binding. Plausible citations cannot manufacture scripture. Historical/news approval requires a reviewable corroboration rubric, distinct supporting source works, exact matching assertions and no contradicting spans. Distinct source IDs alone do not establish independence or truth: the Administrator assessment/reference must document it. Attributed interpretations require evidence and retain disagreements without choosing a uniquely correct philosophy.

The staff page /staff/evidence exposes rubric descriptions, claim history, attribution, review findings, source spans and correction/impact operations. Blazor links to it. Staff policies plus owning tenant membership and action-header protection apply; source audit and denied API audit record changes/gates. Serializable immutable commands provide scoped replay; rowversions reject stale updates.

Core approval, export, shared read/list and the source-use fixture ledger now require current claim eligibility as well as current rights. Exports carry pinned labels and source citations/provenance. Owned staff retain draft/history inspection. Explicit unclaimed Synthetic/Fiction remains labeled as such. Corrections and newly submitted evidence versions invalidate old claim-dependent content and parent-derived revisions immediately; historic Approved metadata is retained for audit and cannot override current eligibility.

Legacy factual/scripture content is retained but receives no automatic M05 qualification. Changed bodies need new links; unchanged bodies copy pinned links. Source-derived ancestry remains conservatively restricted. Source-aware exports cannot be imported by discarding the evidence bundle.

Original self-declared fiction is not a factual verification claim. Listing/span/lineage/impact bounds are in the runbook.
